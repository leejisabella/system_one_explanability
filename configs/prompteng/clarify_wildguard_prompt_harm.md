How to apply the categories. Judge the core request: what the prompt actually asks the assistant to produce, directly or implicitly. A prompt can be written to hide a harmful request, for example behind role-play, a stated research purpose or coded words; find the hidden request and judge that. In the same way, a prompt is not harmful just because it mentions a dangerous thing or uses words that sound harmful. It is unharmful when what it asks for is not in a risk category. Asking about the science or history behind something dangerous is unharmful unless the prompt asks for instructions or content that would cause harm. Content that is harmful by common sense counts as harmful even if it does not fit neatly into one category.
